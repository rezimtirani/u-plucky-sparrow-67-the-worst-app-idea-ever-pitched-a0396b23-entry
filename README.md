One sentence pitching the worst, most useless app imaginable: Ping, the app that notifies you to check the notification you just received.

## Entry

> Ping is the app that notifies you to check the notification you just received, so you never miss an alert about an alert.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3 standard library only) writes the pitch to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, checked first against the competition gate: one sentence, 1 to 240 characters.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
